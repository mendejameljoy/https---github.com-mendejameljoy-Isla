Meet the provincial capital

Deep roots.

On the island of Marinduque, Boac brings together a centuries-old church, a walkable historic center, and traditions that are still part of everyday community life.

This is a place to listen as well as look: ask before taking photographs, support local makers, and make room for the people who call the island home.

Plan a thoughtful visit

Good trips begin with a little local know-how.

Marinduque is an island destination. Confirm transport and local arrangements close to your travel date, and leave room for plans to change.

Getting there

Check ferry schedules ahead. Services, routes, and conditions can change. Confirm current sailings directly with the operator before travelling.

Getting around

Allow extra time around the island. Ask your accommodation or a local tourism office about transport options and arrange rides ahead.

Best timing

Holy Week is a busy time. If visiting for Moriones, plan accommodation early and follow local guidance around religious observances.

Travel gently

Be a considerate guest. Respect sacred spaces, ask before photographing people, and follow local advice at every stop.
